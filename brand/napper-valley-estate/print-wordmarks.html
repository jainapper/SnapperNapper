Display

NAPPER VALLEY ESTATE

Bodoni Moda, capitals only. High contrast and no inscriptional serifs — the luxury reads as spacing, not as carving.

Old style

Napper Valley

Cormorant Garamond Italic, the one setting that breaks capitals — reserved for the softer register.

Locality

MOUNT TAMBORINE HINTERLAND

Jost Light, spaced wide, always subordinate to the name.

Measure

Every setting is justified: the tracking is solved so a line lands on an exact measure, which is why NAPPER, VALLEY and ESTATE stack to identical widths. Never re-track by eye — reset the measure instead.

Word space

The space between words is held open independently of the tracking. Tighten the tracking and the words still read as two.

Artwork

Outlined vector, no live type. Nothing to substitute, nothing to license, nothing to reflow.

In use

The name on the estate

14 / 14

Clear space

The cap height of the N above and below, twice that at the sides. The rules and bars are part of the mark — never crop them.

Minimum size

Stack, Original, Old Style and Label hold to 60 mm wide; Single Rule and Band need 90 mm, being one long line. Below that, set the name in two lines rather than shrinking further.
