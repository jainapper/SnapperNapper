Monogram system · eleven variations

The NVE monogram rebuilt as a family: one set of letterforms, one burgundy world, eleven ways to wear it. Every mark is outlined vector art with no live type, so it can go straight to an umbrella maker, an embroiderer or a foil block without a font licence in sight.

Using them

Rules of the house

Clear space

Keep the height of the N clear on every side. On umbrella panels, double it — the canopy curves away from the eye.

Minimum size

Hairline marks (Heirloom, Seal, Lozenge, Arch) hold to 35 mm wide. Below that use Monoline, Band or Patch.

Colour

Cream on burgundy is the default. Burgundy on cream is the reverse. Brass is a hairline and a foil, never a fill.

Don't

No outlines on the letterforms, no drop shadows, no stretching, no third colour, no rotating the mark off its baseline.

Files

What's in the folder

Each variation ships three SVGs — the burgundy tile, the one-colour cut and the full lockup — plus a 1024 px PNG of the tile.
