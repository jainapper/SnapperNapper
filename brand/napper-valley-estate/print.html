Using them

Rules of the house

15 / 15

Clear space

The height of the N on every side. On umbrella panels double it — the canopy curves away from the eye.

Minimum size

Hairline marks (Heirloom, Seal, Lozenge, Arch) hold to 35 mm wide. Below that use Monoline, Band or Patch.

Colour

Cream on burgundy is the default, burgundy on cream the reverse. Brass is a hairline and a foil, never a fill.

Don't

No outlines on the letterforms, no shadows, no stretching, no third colour, no rotating the mark off its baseline.
