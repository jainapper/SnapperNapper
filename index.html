01About

Built in the UAE.
Trading with the world.

VC Limited is a general trading company headquartered in Dubai — a few kilometres from the ports and free zones that move a meaningful share of world trade.

We work at the practical end of trade: finding reliable supply, verifying it, structuring clean commercial terms and landing goods where they need to be. No layers, no noise — one accountable counterparty from purchase order to proof of delivery.
